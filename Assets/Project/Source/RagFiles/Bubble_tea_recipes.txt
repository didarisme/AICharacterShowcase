THE BOOK OF NIGHT DRINKS
A collection of bubble tea recipes from the old castle

This book has been kept for centuries by the Count, master of the castle on the hill. He gave up drinking blood long ago. Ever since the first tea house opened in the village below, he has collected recipes for tea with pearls. Every recipe is written in his own hand and has a number, a legend, and real ingredients that let the drink be made in the human world too.

THE COUNT'S GENERAL RULES
Cook tapioca pearls according to the package instructions (usually 20–30 minutes in boiling water, then 10–15 minutes covered, off the heat). Transfer the cooked pearls straight into brown sugar syrup so they don't stick together. Syrup: 100 g brown sugar and 100 ml water, simmered for 5 minutes. Pearls are best eaten within 4 hours; after that they harden. The Count calls them "night pearls".
A standard serving is a 500 ml cup. A wide straw is mandatory.


RECIPE No. 1. BLOOD MOON

Legend: The Count's favorite drink. It is served on the night of a full moon, when the moon turns red. Its color is so close to blood that the Count once fooled a party of vampire hunters with it.
Fairy-tale ingredient: juice of the eclipsed moon (in the human world: red dragon fruit purée).
Ingredients:
- 150 ml brewed and chilled hibiscus tea
- 50 g red dragon fruit purée
- 150 ml milk or oat milk
- 20 ml brown sugar syrup
- 60 g tapioca pearls
- ice
Method:
1. Smear the syrup along the inside of the cup to create "blood drips".
2. Put the pearls at the bottom and add ice.
3. Mix the hibiscus tea with the dragon fruit purée and pour it in.
4. Slowly pour the milk on top to create layers.
Taste: sweet and sour, berry-like.


RECIPE No. 2. MIDNIGHT TARO

Legend: The Count grew purple taro root in the castle cellar, where the sun never reaches. It is said this drink helps you dream, even if you haven't slept in centuries.
Fairy-tale ingredient: the underground violet root (in the human world: taro powder).
Ingredients:
- 2 tbsp taro powder
- 200 ml milk
- 100 ml brewed jasmine tea
- 15 ml simple syrup
- 60 g tapioca pearls
- ice
Method:
1. Dissolve the taro powder in a little warm milk.
2. Add the rest of the milk, the tea and the syrup, and whisk.
3. Put the pearls and ice in the cup and pour in the drink.
Taste: creamy, vanilla-nutty.


RECIPE No. 3. BAT TEARS

Legend: The castle bats weep black tears whenever the Count goes away. He learned to serve those tears in a glass.
Fairy-tale ingredient: black tears (in the human world: black tapioca pearls and black sesame paste).
Ingredients:
- 1 tbsp black sesame paste
- 250 ml milk
- 100 ml strong black tea (Assam)
- 20 ml honey
- 70 g black tapioca pearls
- ice
Method:
1. Mix the sesame paste with the honey until smooth.
2. Gradually add the milk and tea, and whisk.
3. Pearls and ice go in the cup first, then the drink.
Taste: rich, nutty, slightly earthy.


RECIPE No. 4. POMEGRANATE KISS

Legend: The pomegranate is the fruit of the underworld. The Count offered it to those he wished to invite into the castle forever.
Fairy-tale ingredient: seeds from the underground garden (in the human world: pomegranate juice and pomegranate popping boba).
Ingredients:
- 150 ml chilled green tea
- 100 ml pomegranate juice
- 15 ml agave syrup
- 60 g pomegranate popping boba (juice-filled pearls)
- a few fresh pomegranate seeds
- ice
Method:
1. Mix the tea, juice and syrup.
2. Put the popping boba and ice in the cup.
3. Pour in the drink and sprinkle fresh pomegranate seeds on top.
Taste: tart and fresh, with pearls that burst in your mouth.


RECIPE No. 5. MIST OVER THE CRYPT

Legend: On autumn nights a thick white mist rises over the family crypt. The Count catches it in a cup and lays it on top of his tea.
Fairy-tale ingredient: crypt mist (in the human world: cheese foam).
Ingredients:
- 250 ml brewed and chilled Earl Grey tea
- 20 ml simple syrup
- 60 g tapioca pearls
- ice
For the "mist" (cheese foam):
- 50 g cream cheese
- 80 ml heavy cream (33%)
- 1 tbsp sugar
- a pinch of salt
Method:
1. Whip the cream cheese, cream, sugar and salt into a thick foam.
2. In the cup: pearls, ice, then tea with syrup.
3. Spoon a thick layer of foam on top. Drink from the rim without stirring.
Taste: bergamot with a salty, creamy foam.


RECIPE No. 6. THE COUNT'S ELIXIR

Legend: A drink the Count serves at his ball once every hundred years. The clear pearls inside look like frozen drops of wine.
Fairy-tale ingredient: century-old wine (in the human world: cherry syrup and cherry juice).
Ingredients:
- 200 ml chilled black tea
- 80 ml cherry juice
- 20 ml cherry syrup
- 60 g crystal boba (agar or konjac pearls)
- 3–4 pitted cherries
- ice
Method:
1. Mix the tea, juice and syrup.
2. Put the crystal boba, cherries and ice in a glass.
3. Pour in the drink. Serve in a tall wine glass, not a cup.
Taste: cherry, slightly tart.


RECIPE No. 7. WILD ROSE AT THE CASTLE GATE

Legend: A wild rose grows by the castle gate and blooms only at night. The Count believes its thorns guard the castle better than any sentry.
Fairy-tale ingredient: night-blooming rosehip (in the human world: rosehip tea and rose syrup).
Ingredients:
- 200 ml rosehip infusion
- 15 ml rose syrup
- 150 ml milk
- 60 g tapioca pearls
- ice
Method:
1. Cool the rosehip infusion and mix it with the rose syrup.
2. In the cup: pearls, ice, infusion.
3. Top up with milk.
Taste: floral, gently sour.


RECIPE No. 8. LAVENDER COBWEB

Legend: Mirta the spider, the oldest resident of the castle library, weaves webs that smell of lavender. The Count drinks this when he reads until dawn.
Fairy-tale ingredient: a thread of lavender cobweb (in the human world: lavender syrup).
Ingredients:
- 200 ml milk
- 100 ml brewed lavender tea
- 15 ml lavender syrup
- 1 tsp honey
- 60 g tapioca pearls
- ice
Method:
1. Mix the tea, milk, syrup and honey.
2. Pearls and ice go in the cup, then pour in the drink.
3. For the "cobweb", drizzle a thin thread of honey in a web pattern on the inside of the cup.
Taste: soothing, floral and honeyed.


RECIPE No. 9. TWILIGHT BLOSSOM

Legend: This drink changes color like the sky at dusk. The Count drinks it at the exact moment the sun sets behind the mountains, when his day begins.
Fairy-tale ingredient: a twilight petal (in the human world: butterfly pea flower tea).
Ingredients:
- 250 ml brewed and chilled butterfly pea tea (blue)
- 20 ml agave syrup
- 20 ml lemon juice (served separately)
- 60 g crystal boba
- ice
Method:
1. Mix the blue tea with the syrup.
2. Pearls and ice go in the cup, then the tea.
3. Pour in the lemon juice in front of the guest: the drink turns from blue to purple.
Taste: light and lemony. The color change is the main attraction.


RECIPE No. 10. VELVET NIGHT

Legend: Named after the Count's velvet cloak, dark red like old wine.
Fairy-tale ingredient: a thread from the cloak (in the human world: beet juice and cocoa).
Ingredients:
- 250 ml milk
- 1 tbsp cocoa powder
- 1 tsp beet juice (for color)
- 20 ml brown sugar syrup
- a pinch of vanilla
- 60 g tapioca pearls
- ice
Method:
1. Dissolve the cocoa in a little hot milk.
2. Add the rest of the milk, the beet juice, syrup and vanilla.
3. Pearls and ice go in the cup, then the drink.
Taste: chocolate and vanilla, like red velvet cake.


RECIPE No. 11. COFFEE OF THE SLEEPLESS CENTURY

Legend: The Count hasn't slept in four hundred years and claims this drink is to blame.
Fairy-tale ingredient: sleeplessness (in the human world: espresso).
Ingredients:
- 1 shot of espresso (30 ml)
- 100 ml strong black tea
- 100 ml condensed or regular milk
- 15 ml brown sugar syrup
- 60 g tapioca pearls
- ice
Method:
1. Mix the tea with the milk and syrup.
2. In the cup: pearls, ice, milk tea.
3. Slowly pour the espresso on top to create a dark layer.
Taste: strong, bittersweet. Similar to Hong Kong yuenyeung.


RECIPE No. 12. EMERALD MOSS

Legend: Emerald moss grows on the stones of the crypt and glows in the dark. The Count gathered it only on moonless nights.
Fairy-tale ingredient: glowing moss (in the human world: matcha).
Ingredients:
- 1.5 tsp matcha powder
- 50 ml hot water (about 80 °C)
- 200 ml milk
- 20 ml brown sugar syrup
- 60 g tapioca pearls
- ice
Method:
1. Whisk the matcha with the water until frothy.
2. Put the pearls with syrup in the cup, then add ice and milk.
3. Pour the matcha on top to create a green layer.
Taste: grassy and creamy, with caramel pearls.


RECIPE No. 13. STARDUST

Legend: A star once fell into the castle well. The Count pulled it out, ground it into dust, and keeps it in a small box, adding a pinch to special drinks.
Fairy-tale ingredient: stardust (in the human world: edible glitter).
Ingredients:
- 200 ml Thai tea (strong spiced tea)
- 100 ml condensed milk or milk with cream
- a pinch of edible glitter
- 60 g tapioca pearls
- ice
Method:
1. Cool the Thai tea and stir in the glitter.
2. In the cup: pearls, ice, tea.
3. Top with condensed milk. Stir before drinking so the "dust" swirls.
Taste: spiced, sweet, bright orange.


RECIPE No. 14. BERRIES FROM THE HILL

Legend: Wild berries grow on the old hill behind the castle. The villagers are afraid to pick them, but the Count makes the freshest drink in the book from them.
Fairy-tale ingredient: forbidden berries (in the human world: a mix of raspberries, blueberries and strawberries).
Ingredients:
- 100 g frozen berries (raspberries, blueberries, strawberries)
- 150 ml chilled green tea
- 50 ml yogurt
- 15 ml honey
- 60 g tapioca pearls
- ice
Method:
1. Blend the berries, tea, yogurt and honey.
2. Pearls go in the cup, with the smoothie on top.
Taste: berry-forward and refreshing.


RECIPE No. 15. CURSED MANGO

Legend: The Count brought this mango back from a distant journey. The merchant warned him the fruit was cursed and would burn his tongue. The Count decided that was the best part.
Fairy-tale ingredient: the curse (in the human world: a pinch of chili and salt).
Ingredients:
- 150 g mango purée
- 150 ml chilled green tea
- 10 ml lime juice
- a pinch of chili powder and a pinch of salt (for the rim)
- 60 g mango popping boba
- ice
Method:
1. Wet the rim of the cup with lime and dip it into the chili-salt mix.
2. Mix the mango, tea and lime juice.
3. In the cup: popping boba, ice, drink.
Taste: sweet, sour and spicy all at once.


RECIPE No. 16. TIGER STRIPES OF ETERNITY

Legend: The Count once kept a tiger. It left dark stripes on the castle walls, and the Count recreated them in a glass.
Fairy-tale ingredient: a tiger stripe (in the human world: thick brown sugar syrup).
Ingredients:
- 250 ml fresh milk (no tea)
- 40 ml thick brown sugar syrup
- 80 g tapioca pearls, cooked directly in the syrup
- ice
Method:
1. Simmer the pearls in the syrup for 5 minutes until the syrup thickens.
2. Tilt the cup and rotate it, smearing the hot syrup along the sides to make "stripes".
3. Add ice and slowly pour in the cold milk.
Taste: caramel and milk. The simplest recipe in the book and the guests' favorite.


RECIPE No. 17. THE VILLAGERS' BREW (FORBIDDEN RECIPE)

Legend: The only recipe in the book that the Count has never tasted and has forbidden in the castle. The villagers drink it "for protection". The Count wrote it down to know his enemy. In the margin, in his hand: "Garlic in tea is barbaric. Ginger alone is enough to keep me away."
Fairy-tale ingredient: a ward (in the human world: ginger and lemon; the modern version contains no garlic).
Ingredients:
- 250 ml hot or chilled black tea
- 2 cm fresh ginger, grated
- 20 ml lemon juice
- 20 ml honey
- 60 g tapioca pearls
Method:
1. Brew the ginger with the tea, steep for 5 minutes, and strain.
2. Add the honey and lemon.
3. Serve hot with pearls, or cool it and add ice.
Taste: spicy, warming, citrusy.


RECIPE No. 18. FOREST FAIRY MILK

Legend: Elian the forest fairy is the only creature of light the Count lets into the castle. She brings green leaves that smell of vanilla and nuts.
Fairy-tale ingredient: fairy leaves (in the human world: pandan).
Ingredients:
- 200 ml coconut milk
- 100 ml water
- 1 tsp pandan extract (or 3 pandan leaves simmered in the water)
- 20 ml simple syrup
- 60 g green or crystal boba
- ice
Method:
1. Mix the coconut milk, water, pandan and syrup.
2. Pearls and ice go in the cup, then the drink.
Taste: coconut and vanilla, tropical.


RECIPE No. 19. LONDON FOG

Legend: The Count brought this drink back from London in 1888, where he spent one very foggy autumn. He never wrote down the details of that trip.
Fairy-tale ingredient: London haze (in the human world: Earl Grey with vanilla and milk foam).
Ingredients:
- 200 ml strong Earl Grey tea
- 100 ml milk
- 15 ml vanilla syrup
- 60 g tapioca pearls
- ice (for the cold version)
Method:
1. Mix the tea with the vanilla syrup.
2. Froth the milk.
3. In the cup: pearls, tea, then milk foam on top. Can be served hot.
Taste: bergamot, vanilla, soft foam.


RECIPE No. 20. THE LAST SUNRISE

Legend: The Count hasn't seen a sunrise in four centuries. This drink is the only sunrise he can allow himself. The layers mirror the sky, from deep red at the horizon to gold above. It is the last recipe in the book, and the Count drinks it only on the longest night of the year.
Fairy-tale ingredient: morning light (in the human world: passion fruit and orange juice).
Ingredients:
- 20 ml grenadine
- 150 ml orange juice
- 100 ml chilled green tea
- 30 g passion fruit purée
- 60 g passion fruit popping boba
- ice
Method:
1. Pour the grenadine into the bottom and add the popping boba.
2. Fill the cup with ice.
3. Mix the orange juice, tea and passion fruit, and pour slowly over a spoon so the grenadine stays at the bottom.
Taste: citrusy and tropical. It looks like a sunrise in a glass.
